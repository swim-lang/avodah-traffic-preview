Reckless Driving Defense

In Virginia, this may be more than a ticket.

Virginia law can classify reckless driving as a criminal misdemeanor. Some charges are based on speed. Others arise from different driving allegations. The exact code section, alleged facts, court and driving history all matter.

Why the Misdemeanor Label Matters

Different from an ordinary infraction.

Virginia law generally treats reckless driving as a Class 1 misdemeanor. The possible consequences are different from those of an ordinary infraction and may include criminal, license and employment concerns. Case-specific advice is required — this page links to the current statute rather than listing outcomes.

Common Situations

High speed on an interstate or highway

Start with the alleged speed, limit, location and measurement information on the summons.

Out-of-state driver

A Virginia case may interact with the driver's home-state record and appearance obligations. Those effects require state-specific review.

CDL or work-related driving

Tell counsel immediately if the license supports your job, even if the charge occurred in a personal vehicle.

Young driver or parent inquiry

Identify the driver's age, license state and court date. The driver is the prospective client, and intake should reflect that.
