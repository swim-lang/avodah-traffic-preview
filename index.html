Serious Traffic Matters

Three details make the first conversation more useful.

The charge

Use the exact words and code section on the summons, warrant or other paperwork. Similar descriptions can carry different consequences.

The court

Virginia traffic matters are local and court-driven. The locality can affect scheduling, preparation and appearance questions.

The date

Your court date and any administrative or license deadline should be identified immediately. Do not assume contacting a lawyer changes a deadline.

Counsel for the Consequence

A driving matter can reach work, licensing and daily life.

For some drivers, the concern is not only a fine. A charge may affect a commercial license, a professional role, security-sensitive work, insurance, an out-of-state driving record or the ability to keep working and caring for family. Tell counsel what is at stake so the case can be evaluated in context.
