# Traffic launch readiness

The GitHub Pages build is an unindexed review preview. It is not the production intake system and must not be treated as launched.

## Completed preview checks

- Every route remains `noindex, nofollow`, and `robots.txt` blocks the preview root.
- Open Graph and X/Twitter card metadata are present for link-preview testing.
- Draft Insights links and homepage teasers are hidden until Avodah completes attorney review and gives written publication approval.
- Service, attorney, contact, disclaimer and privacy routes remain available for review.

## Production inputs still required

- Avodah must approve the public Traffic CallRail number, its routing destination and the intake owner. Do not infer approval from older or conflicting numbers on existing properties.
- Avodah must approve secure form recipients, the form provider and the production privacy language.
- Confirm the final host, attach the approved domain and configure encrypted production environment variables.
- Keep one authoritative public number for the property. Test every call path from an outside phone before launch.
- Do not publish a mailbox or virtual-office address or use one for a Google Business Profile. Any dedicated location must be staffed by Avodah, display permanent signage and receive clients during its stated hours.
- Add canonical and `og:url` tags only after the final HTTPS production domain is attached. Generate the production sitemap after the route review.
- Remove preview `noindex` controls only through a separately approved production launch.

## Editorial gate

The Insights landing page and draft articles remain inventory only. Keep them out of navigation and `noindex` until each page completes attorney review, source review and written publication approval.
