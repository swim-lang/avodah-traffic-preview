Privacy notice

Avodah Traffic collects only the information you choose to provide through the case-review form. That information is sent to Avodah's intake team so the firm can review your request and determine whether it can speak with you.

Submitting information does not create an attorney-client relationship. Do not send confidential documents or a detailed account until Avodah confirms that it can speak with you. The site also uses privacy-conscious website analytics to understand general traffic and completed actions. Legal intake details are not sent to analytics.

For questions about information submitted through this site, contact Avodah using the phone number or contact form shown on this website.
