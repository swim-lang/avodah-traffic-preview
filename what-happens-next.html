The Process

Turn the paperwork into a plan.

The process begins with a few confirmed facts. From there, counsel can identify what needs attention now, what information may matter and what the client should expect before court.

Begin the First Step
Six steps
01 Identifiers 02 Conflicts 03 Engagement 04 Information 05 Preparation 06 Court
Step 01

Share the identifiers

Send the charge as written, code section if available, court or locality, court date, license state and contact information. Include whether a CDL, professional license or security-sensitive job may be affected.

Step 02

Conflict and fit review

Avodah determines whether it can discuss the matter and whether the charge is within the practice. Sending a form does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Step 03

Scope and engagement

If Avodah can help, the firm explains the proposed scope, fee arrangement, responsibilities and next step.

Step 04

Case information

After engagement, counsel may request the summons, driving record, relevant documents, recordings, photographs or other information. The exact request depends on the charge.

Step 05

Preparation

Mark and the client identify court responsibilities, evidence questions, preparation tasks and communication. Do not assume that counsel can appear without the client unless Mark confirms it for the specific matter.

Step 06

Court and follow-through

After any hearing or resolution, the client should understand orders, payments, license steps, deadlines and documents that need to be retained.

While You Wait

What clients can do now.

Keep every page of the paperwork
Calendar the court date and arrive only according to confirmed instructions
Preserve relevant information without changing it
Avoid discussing the matter publicly or on social media
Do not contact witnesses or attempt to create evidence based on website advice
Ask counsel before taking a step that could affect the case
